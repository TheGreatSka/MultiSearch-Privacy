MultiSearch Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

MultiSearch is a Chrome extension that helps users search multiple video game marketplaces and price guides from one convenient interface.

Information MultiSearch stores

MultiSearch stores user-selected preferences, saved searches, and search history using Chrome's extension storage APIs. These settings are used only to provide the extension's features.

Information MultiSearch sends

When you perform a search, MultiSearch opens the websites you selected and sends your search query to those websites so their normal search functionality can be used. MultiSearch does not operate a separate server or central database for these searches.

Data sharing

MultiSearch does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties for advertising or profiling. Search queries are sent directly to the third-party websites the user chooses to search, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Analytics and tracking

MultiSearch does not include its own analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking system.

Accounts

MultiSearch does not require a MultiSearch account.

Data retention and deletion

Saved searches and search history remain in Chrome extension storage until the user deletes them, resets MultiSearch, uninstalls the extension, or otherwise clears the relevant Chrome extension data. Synced preferences are handled by Chrome's storage sync service when Chrome Sync is enabled.

Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, use the support information provided on the MultiSearch Chrome Web Store listing.

Changes

This policy may be updated if MultiSearch's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
